# Searching and Filtering Designs

Use [Browse](#/browse) to find digital embroidery designs by keyword, designer, hoop size, dimensions, or tags.

---

## Search using keywords and operators

Type into the search box at the top of [Browse](#/browse):

- **Keywords**: `rose floral` (matches designs containing both words).
- **Exact phrase**: `"cross stitch"` (matches the exact phrase).
- **Exclusion**: `-applique` (excludes designs containing that word).
- **OR search**: `rose OR tulip` (matches designs containing either term).
- **Wildcard**: `*.pes` or `flower*.jef` (matches specific file patterns).
- **Search scopes**: Tick or untick **File name**, **Folder name**, or **Tags** beneath the search box to restrict where the query looks.

To cancel a running search on a large catalogue, press <kbd>Escape</kbd> or click the red **Cancel** button in the search bar. Click the **✕** button to clear the search text.

---

## Filter by designer, tags, or hoops

Click **Additional Filters** to expand the filter panel:

1. **Designer & Source**: Tick specific designers or acquisition shops.
2. **Image & Stitching Tags**: Tick subject tags (e.g. *Animals*, *Holidays*) or stitch techniques (e.g. *In The Hoop*, *Lace*).
3. **Hoop size & Dimensions**:
   - Choose a specific machine hoop frame.
   - Enter **Min** or **Max** dimensions in millimetres (e.g. Max Height = `80` matches designs up to 80 mm).
4. **Verification & Status**:
   - Filter by **Verified only** or **Unverified only**.
   - Filter by **Stitched** or **Not Stitched**.
   - Filter by star rating (1★ to 5★).

Active filter criteria appear as chips beneath the search box. Click the **✕** on any chip to remove it, or click **Clear all** to reset all filters.

---

## Sort your results

Use the **Sort by** and **Direction** dropdowns in the top-right:

- **Date Added**: Order by import date (newest or oldest first).
- **Date Updated**: Order by recent edits, ratings, or tag changes.
- **Folder**: Groups designs by their containing folder on disk. Within each folder, designs are always ordered alphabetically by filename.
- **Name**: Alphabetical order by filename (A–Z or Z–A).
- **Rating**: Star rating order.
- **Stitched**: Stitched designs first.

---

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Search returns no results | Check for spelling errors or active filter chips that may be restricting results. Click **Clear all** in the filter chips row. |
| You want to find designs needing attention | In **Additional Filters**, use the **Needs attention** dropdown to find designs missing preview images, exceeding hoop area, or master outline files needing export. |
